# Legal Disclaimer & Acceptable Use Policy

## 1. Intended Purpose
FastDownloader is a general-purpose, open-source download acceleration manager and network client utility designed to:
- Accelerate authorized downloads of open-access files, software distributions, open datasets, and user-owned files via multi-connection parallel chunk streaming.
- Manage sequential download queues, scheduling, and local network transfers.
- Provide assistive capture and media archival for content that users have explicit copyright authorization or licensing to download.
